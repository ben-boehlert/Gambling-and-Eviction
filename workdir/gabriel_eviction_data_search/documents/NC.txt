North Carolina Judicial Branch - Data and Statistics

The NC Administrative Office of the Courts (AOC) publishes eviction (summary
ejectment, SUME) statistics in two forms:

1) OpenDataSoft-based data portal: data.nccourts.gov
   Three key datasets (each has CSV/JSON/API export):

   a) Caseload Inventory - caseload statistics per fiscal year (July 1 - June 30)
      including cases filed, disposed, and pending. County-level.
      https://data.nccourts.gov/explore/dataset/caseload-inventory/

   b) Caseload Ranked Criminal Charge / Civil Issue / Civil Order Result
      https://data.nccourts.gov/explore/dataset/caseload-ranked-event/
      Breaks out civil issue types — summary ejectment (SUME) is one of the
      civil issue categories explicitly tracked.

   c) Caseload Dispositions
      https://data.nccourts.gov/explore/dataset/caseload-disposition/

   Case Statistics Dashboard (landing):
      https://data.nccourts.gov/pages/dashboard/
      Sections: Overview, Criminal, Civil, Compare. Civil section breaks out
      summary ejectment as a distinct issue type with filed/disposed/pending
      counts at the statewide and county level.

   Frequency: FISCAL YEAR (July 1 - June 30). Not monthly in the public portal.
   For monthly/fiscal-period granularity the AOC uses 13 "judicial periods" per
   year internally, but the public dataset aggregates to fiscal year.

2) PDF publications (nccourts.gov):
   - Civil Overall Caseload Activity Report
     https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/publications/civil-overall-caseload-activity-report
     County-level and statewide filed/pending/disposed civil cases, fiscal year.

   - Civil Issue Filings / Order Results
     https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/publications/civil-issue-filingsorder-results
     Breakouts by civil issue (including SUME) by county.

   - North Carolina Courts Statistical and Operational Reports
     https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/publications/north-carolina-courts-statistical-and-operational-reports
     Historical archive.

   - Judicial Branch Annual Reports (FY2019-20 through FY2023-24 available).

Geographic granularity: 100 NC counties + statewide aggregates.
Temporal coverage: Multiple fiscal years, with at least FY2019-20 through
  FY2023-24 confirmed. Historical SUME series likely extends to early 2000s in
  the Statistical and Operational Reports archive.

Access format: OpenDataSoft API + CSV/Excel download from data.nccourts.gov;
  PDFs from nccourts.gov publications pages.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT: the existing Gambling-and-Eviction project notes that NC has
  monthly county-level filings in Excel (see CLAUDE.md: "NC AOC publishes
  monthly county-level filings in Excel"). This monthly granularity is NOT
  advertised in the standard public dashboard (which is fiscal-year only). The
  monthly Excel files are likely either (a) older AOC "Civil Overall Caseload
  Activity" monthly reports that predate the new dashboard launch, or (b) the
  source files compiled into the existing repo's ETS panel. Worth direct outreach
  to the NC AOC Research and Planning Division to confirm whether a monthly
  county-level SUME extract is available for 2016-present.

Procedural note: in NC, summary ejectment cases are filed in Small Claims
  Magistrate Court (not District or Superior Court civil divisions). Appeals go
  to District Court. The SUME issue code tracks small claims filings.
