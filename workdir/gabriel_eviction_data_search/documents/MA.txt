Massachusetts Civil Court Reports and Dashboards (Trial Court / Department of
Research and Planning)

The Massachusetts Trial Court publishes residential summary process (eviction)
statistics via two mutually reinforcing products:

1) Ch. 239 s. 15 Monthly Report (statutorily mandated, published monthly)
   URL pattern: https://www.mass.gov/doc/ch-239-s-15-monthly-report-{month-YYYY}/download
   Example: https://www.mass.gov/doc/ch-239-s-15-monthly-report-november-2025/download

   PDF with 9 pages of tables:
     Page 1: Summary process cases FILED by court department (Boston Municipal,
             District Court, Housing Court) by MONTH
     Page 2: Default judgments by summary process action / type
     Page 3: Executions for possession orders granted
     Page 4: Continuances due to pending emergency rental assistance applications
     Page 5: Stays due to pending applications
     Page 6: Average length of continuances and stays
     Page 7: Stays requested/granted/denied per Ch. 239 s. 9 and 10
     Page 8: Pre-trial mediation participation and outcomes
     Page 9: On-site legal representation and court diversion

   Coverage: January 2024 - present (new reporting regime triggered by Ch. 239
     s. 15 amendments). Each month's report shows full history since Jan 2024.
   Granularity: Court DEPARTMENT (3 departments), not county, on most pages.

2) Tableau Public Dashboards (author: drap4687 = Dept of Research and Planning)
   URL: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/drap4687
   Key dashboards:
   - "Massachusetts Trial Court, Summary Process"
     https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/drap4687/viz/MassachusettsTrialCourtSummaryProcess/SummaryProcess
   - "Summary Process Executions Issued"
     https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/drap4687/viz/MassachusettsTrialCourtSummaryProcessExecutionsIssued/ExecutionsIssd_byWeekMonth
   - "Charges Dashboard" (criminal — not relevant)

   Per mass.gov description: "residential summary process cases filed and
   executions issued in calendar years 2020 to 2025, aggregated by YEAR, MONTH,
   and COUNTY, including cases from the Boston Municipal, District, and Housing
   Courts." Updated WEEKLY. All data sourced from MassCourts (Trial Court case
   management system).

   Download: click download icon, choose "crosstab", select Excel (.xlsx) or CSV.

3) Fiscal Year Statistics: Trial Court Statistics for FY 2002-2023 (annual totals
   by Trial Court Department, case type, county)
   Example: https://www.mass.gov/lists/trial-court-statistics-for-fiscal-year-2023

Summary data visible in Nov 2025 PDF:
  Filings by month, Jan 2024-Oct 2025 (22 months). District Court dominates
  (~2,500-3,700/month), Housing Court second (~400-570/month), Boston Municipal
  smallest (~30-70/month).

Coverage years for monthly breakouts:
  - Ch. 239 monthly reports: Jan 2024 - present (court department only, no county)
  - Tableau dashboard: CY 2020 - 2025 (county-level, monthly), updated weekly
  - Earlier: FY stats by case type 2002-2023 (annual totals only)

Geographic structure: MA evictions flow through three court departments
  overlapping in jurisdiction — District Court (most counties), Housing Court
  (covers most of the state through regional divisions), and Boston Municipal
  Court (Suffolk/Boston). A given eviction may be heard in any of these depending
  on venue choice. County-level aggregation available via Tableau dashboards.
