NJ Courts Statistics Page (njcourts.gov/public/statistics)

This is the landing page for the New Jersey Judiciary's publicly available caseload
statistics. It hosts both interactive dashboards and PDF reports.

Interactive dashboards (Tableau-based, require JS):
  - "Filings and Resolutions Dashboard - Current" (court year-to-date, updated monthly)
  - "Filings and Resolutions Dashboard - Court Year" (historical full court years)
  - "Backlog Dashboard - Current" and "Court Year"
  Dashboards allow filtering by case type (landlord-tenant IS a case type in Special
  Civil Part) and by county/vicinage. Updated monthly. Court year runs July-June.

PDF reports:
  - Court Management Statistics (monthly; county/vicinage breakdowns)
  - Civil Statistics Report (Superior Court, includes Special Civil Part filings
    and resolutions by case type — landlord-tenant is a Special Civil Part case type)
  - Municipal Court Statistics
  - Superior Court Caseload Reference Guide, 2018-2022
  Reports historically available from July 2004 to June 2025.

Geographic granularity: 15 vicinages (each covers 1-3 counties) and statewide totals.
  NJ has 21 counties but courts are organized into 15 vicinages.

Frequency: Monthly for dashboards and Court Management PDFs; fiscal year cumulative
  for the Civil Statistics Reports.

Coverage: Landlord-tenant is the largest category in Special Civil Part. NJ Special
  Civil Part handles all residential & commercial L/T cases statewide (not split
  across small claims or housing court).

Directive 15-23 (Aug 2023) materially changed filing procedures — LCIS (Landlord
Case Information Statement) now required — which may create a break in series.

Access method: Dashboards (Tableau, interactive, manual download of underlying data
may require selecting crosstab export); PDF reports downloadable from the statistics
page. No documented bulk API or CSV feed.
