DC Superior Court — Landlord-Tenant (eviction) filings data

BOTTOM LINE: DC does NOT publish bulk eviction filing data. This is confirmed by a recent (2025) New America brief that investigated DC eviction data availability and found:

  "Bulk data on eviction filings and eviction judgments is not accessible in DC, and the
   Office of the Tenant Advocate's monthly data only includes aggregate numbers."

The New America brief built an automated scraper (with January Advisors) to collect individual LT case records from the DC Courts public portal at:
  https://portal-dc.tylertech.cloud/Portal
This is DC's Tyler-Odyssey public-access portal. Bulk export is not offered; individual dockets must be pulled case-by-case via the portal.

Access options for researchers:

1. DC Courts public case search portal (Odyssey / Tyler Tech):
   - https://portal-dc.tylertech.cloud/Portal
   - Per-case docket lookup; no bulk export.
   - Access is read-only and anonymous (no account required for most data).
   - Landlord-Tenant cases are a named case type.

2. DC Courts legacy eAccess portal:
   https://eaccess.dccourts.gov/eaccess/home.page

3. DC Courts Annual Reports (aggregate year-end summaries only):
   https://www.dccourts.gov/about/organizational-performance/annual-reports
   - 2016–2023 annual reports exist; no 2024 report as of search date.
   - Contain annual LT filing totals at the court (DC-wide) level.
   - PDF narrative reports; no monthly breakdown; not machine-readable.

4. Formal data request to DC Courts (Office of Research):
   - Required for non-public microdata.
   - Form A (Public): for non-government researchers.
   - Form B (Non-Public): for DC/federal government entities.
   - Historical precedent: academics have obtained LT microdata via this channel (DCist 2020 piece used such data).

5. Office of the Tenant Advocate (OTA) — Scheduled Evictions:
   https://ota.dc.gov/page/scheduled-evictions
   - Monthly aggregate counts of *completed/scheduled* evictions (not filings).
   - Different concept from filings — captures writ execution, not case initiation.

6. Landlord-Tenant Clerk's Office contact:
   - Phone: (202) 879-4879
   - Email: landlordandtenantdocket@dcsc.gov

Historical context (from searches):
  - Filings fell from ~48,000 (2003) to ~31,000 (2017).
  - DC has a single Superior Court; geographic unit is effectively DC-wide (no sub-city breakdown in published statistics).

Notable precedents:
  - Eviction Lab has DC data in the ETS / national eviction database (via scraping and/or data request).
  - Urban Institute ("DC's Eviction Filings Are Down"): presumably scraped or data-requested source.
  - New America / January Advisors (2025): custom scraper on Tyler portal.

Frequency of any PUBLIC data:
  - Annual (DC Courts Annual Reports)
  - Monthly (OTA Scheduled Evictions, but "scheduled" != "filed")
  - Daily/real-time (per-case lookup only, no bulk)

For the Gambling-and-Eviction project:
  - DC legalized online sports betting May 2020 (GambetDC / later FanDuel partnership 2024).
  - DC is NOT in the ETS monthly panel.
  - Getting DC into a monthly panel would require (a) scraping the Tyler portal (political/legal risk; ToS review needed), (b) formal data request to DC Courts Office of Research, or (c) coordinating with Eviction Lab to acquire their DC data.
  - DC is a SINGLE observation (one jurisdiction) so the value-add for a 24-state panel is marginal; better used for case-study / descriptive context.
