District Court of Maryland Eviction Case Data (Maryland Open Data Portal, Socrata)

Official state judiciary dataset published on the Maryland Open Data Portal under
the Housing category. Required by 2022 MD law; District Court began collecting the
statutorily mandated fields on 2023-01-01, public dashboard launched May 2023.

Dataset metadata (as of 2026-04-15):
  Title: District Court of Maryland Eviction Case Data
  Host: opendata.maryland.gov (Socrata)
  Dataset ID: mvqb-b4hf
  Rows: 662,562
  Updated: most recent record 2026-01-31
  Temporal coverage: January 2023 - February 2026
  Category: Housing
  Fields (15): Event Date, Event Type, Event Comment, County, Location, Tenant City,
    Tenant State, Tenant ZIP, Case Type, Case Number, Evicted Date, + year fields.

Event Type distribution (approx):
  Petition filing: 381,809
  Warrant cancellation: 190,575
  Actual evictions (warrant executed): 58,231

Case Type breakdown:
  Failure to Pay Rent (FPR): 638,629 (dominant)
  Other: Tenant Holding Over, Breach of Lease, Wrongful Detainer, Grantor in Possession

Geographic granularity: County (all 23 counties + Baltimore City). Baltimore County
  + Baltimore City together account for 360,634 cases. Location field also lists
  specific District Court locations.

Access: Socrata API (JSON, CSV, XML), direct CSV download, OData, visualization UI.
  Bulk CSV download one click from dataset page. No rate limits for CSV export.

Critical caveat from MD Courts: "The data will not match the monthly eviction totals
  that are seen on the District Court of Maryland Landlord/Tenant Case Activity
  Report. That data is collected manually by all jurisdictions, as of the end of
  each month. Because the data provided in the spreadsheet relies on data entry,
  the clerks may not have all data entered into the case management system when
  the report is run."

Secondary source: District Court of Maryland Landlord/Tenant Case Activity Report
  — monthly, manually compiled by each jurisdiction, hosted via Maryland Courts
  website. Predates 2023 in some form; use as a longer-history cross-reference.

Pre-2023 data: Not in the Socrata dataset. For 2016-2022 coverage one must fall
  back to (a) the monthly L/T Case Activity Report PDFs from the Maryland Judiciary
  annual Statistical Abstracts (mdcourts.gov/publications/annualreports), or (b)
  the Maryland Judiciary Data Dashboard (https://www.mdcourts.gov/dashboard/about
  — notes that total dispositions for landlord-tenant cases became available FY25+
  due to phased MDEC rollout).
