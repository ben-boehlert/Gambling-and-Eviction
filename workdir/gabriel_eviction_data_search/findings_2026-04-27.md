# Monthly Eviction Data Scouting Findings

Date: 2026-04-27

Scope: official or official-adjacent monthly/case-level eviction filing data that can
fill gaps in `data/raw/state_month_panel_with_treatment.csv` and the LSC/ETS county
monthly file.

## Baseline Check

- Current local `data/raw/monthly_county_data_download.csv` matches the current LSC
  monthly county download exactly: 120,357 rows, 2016-01 through 2025-09.
- Current LSC monthly state/county files add no states that are absent from the
  local state panel.
- Local state panel has 38 states. States absent: AL, CA, DC, IA, IL, MD, MI, NE,
  NJ, OR, SD, WA, WY.

## Recommended Acquisition Order

1. OR: pull/download Evicted in Oregon tables or email PSU for raw monthly county/state filing data.
2. NJ: extract Court Management/Civil Statistics dashboard or file OPRA for monthly landlord-tenant filings by vicinage/county.
3. RI: pull RIHousing dashboard data to fill the 2020-present gap.
4. MA: pull Trial Court dashboard data to extend MA and cross-check local MA series.
5. MD: collect historical Landlord/Tenant Case Activity PDF reports before using the 2023+ Socrata case data.
6. WA: send AOC request for monthly superior-court unlawful-detainer filings by county, 2016-present.
7. Annual backup: CA, WY, IL, MI, NE for an annual robustness panel.

## Key Caveats

- Public LSC/CCDI is already current locally; it does not solve absent-state coverage.
- OR and RI are not court-hosted raw APIs, but both cite direct court data.
- MD Socrata measures events on eviction cases and starts too late for a clean MD pre-period.
- WA public caseload reports are monthly and statewide, but the public tables do not isolate eviction filings.
- CA has excellent annual unlawful-detainer CSV data, but not monthly historical data.
