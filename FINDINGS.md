## 2. PAP Decision Table (Shift12, NV-Free, Spliced Window)

Decision rule: **size gate first** (any estimator whose null rejection rate is materially off 5% is disqualified), then MDE among survivors picks the spec.

**Two levels, both main specs (standing decision, 2026-09-14).** The state-month panel and the county-month panel are run as **two co-equal headline specifications**, kept separate and never pooled. Neither is "the robustness arm". They answer the same question on different samples and neither dominates: the state arm has **35 clusters** on the 42-state panel and admits every non-LSC source (MD NH WI MI OR + the 7 Eviction Lab states); the county arm has **26 clusters and 1,180 units**, gains the counties a state's fixed basket drops (Miami-Dade, Harris, Rutherford, Ada, Kanawha), and loses every non-LSC state. Clusters bind, not observations. Each level carries its own size gate and its own MDE, and a spec is reported as headline only for the level it was gated on — a county cell must never be cited as a state MDE, or the reverse. Arm accounting: `data/processed/county_vs_state_arm_summary.csv` (cite the `states`/`units` columns only; `switchers` is 0 in every row, a loader artifact).

### ⚠ PENDING DESIGN SWITCH — launch-indexed 6–18 window, 3-month baseline, June 2022 start (2026-09-18)

**The three changes, and why:**

1. **Sample start moves to 2022-06** (from the 2021-07 COVID-drop boundary; supersedes an interim 2022-03 proposal). Two independent reasons:

## 5. Standing Design Facts & Traps

- **The (18,0) eligibility window nearly doubles the LP-DiD sampling SD (2026-09-16).** On the identical 42-state panel and identical 24T/11C placement, `min_pre 18 / min_post 0` gives δ=0 SD 0.0628 vs 0.0337 under the legacy `(12, 6)`. `min_post: 0` admits switchers contributing as little as one of the 13 pooled outcome months, and those near-empty contributions are noise: the placement probe (`analysis/diagnostics/eligibility_window_placement_probe.R`) shows (18,0) turns ~4.5 dead slots per rep into thin contributors whose one to three outcome months sit at the COVID edges (2020-01/02 or 2021-08..10) or a series end, while total usable horizons barely move (114 → 119) and contributing switchers rise 11.8 → 16.3. Neither rule guarantees the estimator's inputs: (12,6) guarantees 7 outcome months but none of the baseline (41% of placed switchers dead), (18,0) guarantees the baseline span but one outcome month. A placement rule cannot fix this. **DECIDED 2026-09-16 (Ben): the switcher inclusion rule** — full 6-month pre-launch baseline observed, at least **K = 7** usable outcome months in [launch+12, launch+24], with the **3 months on each side of the COVID drop excluded as outcome months** for everyone. One rule, applied identically in placement (`eligibility: presence` — placed == contributing), in the estimator (`lpdid_min_horizons`, `lpdid_excluded_months`), and in the real-data script (`MIN_HORIZONS=7 SPLICE_BUFFER=3`). **AMENDED 2026-09-17 (Ben): the baseline is held to the same splice standard as the outcome** — a month too close to the COVID splice to measure an outcome is too close to anchor one. As first written the exclusion applied only to outcome months while the baseline was a pure presence test, which was the rule's one internal inconsistency. Exactly one switcher is affected: AR (launch 2022-03, baseline [2021-09, 2022-02], of which 2021-09/10 are splice-excluded). AR's own filings there are fine — 2021-09 is +17% on its neighbours, broad-based across 9 of 12 basket counties, and the 164%-of-historical-average reading that first looked alarming is an artifact of LSC's FIXED pre-2020 baseline (verified: Pulaski County's September denominator is 168.8 in every year 2016–2025). The reason to exclude AR is the rule, not the data: its baseline sits where the control pool is most dispersed, and unwind exposure predicts the placebo long-difference SD monotonically (6 unwind months 0.222 AR, 4 0.167 KS, 2 0.160 MD, 0 0.107 for the other seven). Under the amended rule **9 of 26 launched states are switchers (KS MA MD ME NC OH KY DE VT)**, every one of them now below the marginal-cluster break-even threshold (max SD 0.167 vs threshold 0.181), pooled placebo SE 0.0463 → 0.0443, then 0.0483 after ME briefly dropped, 0.0442 once ME returned, and **0.0504** once Idaho was verified and un-excluded (a deliberate precision cost — see the ledger's `idaho_statewide_verification` row), and the COVID-drop-window choice becomes irrelevant to it (0.0443 / 0.0443 / 0.0447 across the three candidate ends). Knob: `baseline_clean` (config) / `lpdid_baseline_clean` (spec) / `BASELINE_CLEAN` (real-data env), all default TRUE; hashed wherever `splice_buffer_months > 0`, so every k7-family scenario id changes and no post-change run can resume a pre-change partial. Memo: [`docs/findings-archive/2026-09-17_splice-clean-baseline.md`](docs/findings-archive/2026-09-17_splice-clean-baseline.md). Superseded list (pre-amendment): 10 of 26 (AR KS MA MD ME NC OH KY DE VT); the D1 template is restricted to them (`template_states`). Module: `R/power/inclusion_rule.R`; real set: `analysis/diagnostics/inclusion_rule_real_switchers.R`. **First gates under the rule (2026-09-16) are design-infeasible, not evidence:** with the template restricted to the 9 real cohorts (a 24-month span) and the anchor free, placement lands at the real 2022–2024 dates in 36 of 40 reps, where the only untreated states with data are the eight never-treated long-series states (AK GA MN MO OK SC TX UT) — they become the pseudo-switchers in nearly every rep and 0–5 controls remain (the blinded panel truncates real switchers at launch: 28 untreated states in 2019, 14 in 2022, 8 in 2024). LP-DiD SD 0.215 with a cluster of reps at +1.06 is that degeneracy, not the estimator. **Standing trap: a pseudo-treatment design cannot reproduce the real post-COVID design at its own dates, because the real design's control pool IS the never-treated set.** Open decision (Ben): (A) presence rule + template slid into the pre-COVID era (random feasible anchor, late cohorts dropped), MDE reported with the pre/post era-variance inflation the 08-19 memo prescribes; (B) shrink the template to what the post-COVID pool supports (~4 switchers with ≥4 controls) and accept the understatement; (C) treat the post-COVID design as un-simulable and rest its power claim on the analytic/wild inference validated on the pre-COVID placement. Do not compare any (18,0) run to the (12,6) p39 headline without this adjustment; the PAP eligibility rule is an open decision, not a settled correction. - **Unweighted county-level estimation weights states by LSC county count (2026-09-16).** Each county counts once, so KY (120) outweighs KS (1) 120:1 on both the treated and control side. Any county-level LP-DiD/TWFE/did2s number computed without `lpdid_unit_weights: renters` (or an equivalent) is a county-count-weighted estimand.
