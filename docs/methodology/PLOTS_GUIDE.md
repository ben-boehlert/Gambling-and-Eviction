## Key Plots for Understanding Results

### 1. **Pre-Trends Comparison** ⭐ MOST IMPORTANT
**File:** `mortgage_delinquency_out/comparison/pretrends_comparison.png`

**What it shows:** Only the pre-treatment periods (before online gambling legalization) for all specifications.

**How to interpret:**
- ✅ **Good (valid):** Lines should be flat and centered at zero
- ❌ **Bad (invalid):** Lines that slope, trend, or deviate systematically from zero

**What we see:**
- **Narrow window (black line with ✓):** Relatively flat at zero → Valid parallel trends
- **Other specs (colored lines):** Show clear trends away from zero → Pre-trends violations

**Conclusion:** Only the narrow window specification has valid parallel trends assumption.

---

### 2. **Raw Data Trends by Treatment Status**
**File:** `mortgage_delinquency_out/comparison/raw_trends_by_treatment.png`

**What it shows:** Average delinquency rates over time for:
- Red line: States that eventually legalized online gambling (24 states)
- Blue line: States that never legalized (27 states)

**How to interpret:**
- Lines should track closely before 2018 (first treatment) for parallel trends
- Divergence after 2018 could indicate treatment effect

**What we see:**
- Pre-2018: Lines have **different slopes** → Pre-trends violation
- Treated states (red) had systematically different trajectories throughout
- Both groups peaked during 2008-2010 financial crisis

**Conclusion:** Visual evidence of pre-trends violation - treated states were different before treatment.

---

### 3. **Event Study Comparison**
**File:** `mortgage_delinquency_out/comparison/event_study_comparison.png`

**What it shows:** All five specifications overlaid on common ±12 month window.

**How to interpret:**
- X-axis: Months relative to treatment (negative = before, positive = after)
- Y-axis: Estimated effect on log delinquency rate
- Lines should be flat before treatment (e < 0)
- Vertical line at -0.5: Treatment occurs at e = 0

**What we see:**
- All specs show some pre-treatment variation
- Post-treatment effects are small and imprecisely estimated
- No clear treatment effect visible in any specification

## Individual Specification Plots

Each specification has its own event study plot in:
`mortgage_delinquency_out/specifications/[spec_name]/twfe_event_study.png`

### 4. Baseline: Full Period 2008-2025
**Window:** ±24 months
**Result:** ❌ FAIL (F=9,405, p<0.001)

**What we see:**
- Strong systematic pattern in pre-treatment coefficients
- Clear downward trend from e=-24 to e=-15
- Cannot use for causal inference

---

### 5. Exclude COVID Period
**Window:** ±24 months (drops March 2020 - July 2021)
**Result:** ❌ FAIL (F=36, p<0.001)

**What we see:**
- Removing COVID reduces F-stat but doesn't fix the problem
- Still shows significant pre-treatment patterns
- COVID not the main driver of pre-trends violation

---

### 6. Post-Financial Crisis (2012+)
**Window:** ±24 months (drops 2008-2011)
**Result:** ❌ FAIL (F=9,405, p<0.001)

**What we see:**
- Identical F-statistic to baseline
- Financial crisis period not driving the violation
- Issue is structural, not event-specific

---

### 7. Narrow Window (±12 months) ✅
**Window:** ±12 months
**Result:** ✅ **PASS** (F=1.73, p=0.063)

**What we see:**
- Pre-treatment coefficients cluster near zero
- No systematic trend in pre-treatment period
- **This is the only valid specification**
- Can make causal claims only for ±12 month window

**Limitation:** Shorter window means less ability to detect long-run effects

---

### 8. Pre-2020 Adopters Only
**Window:** ±24 months (only 8 early-adopting states)
**Result:** ❌ FAIL (F=555,709,655, p<0.001)

**What we see:**
- **Extreme F-statistic** (555 million!)
- Early adopters have fundamentally different pre-trends
- Strong evidence of selection into treatment
- Should NOT be used for analysis

**Warning:** VCOV matrix issues indicate numerical problems

## Technical Notes

### Plot Aesthetics
- **✓ symbol:** Marks specifications that pass pre-trends test
- **Error bars:** 95% confidence intervals (clustered at state level)
- **Dashed horizontal line:** Zero (no effect)
- **Dotted vertical line:** Treatment date (e=0)

### Colors in Comparison Plots
- Red: Baseline specification
- Blue: No COVID specification
- Green: Post-2012 specification
- **Black:** Narrow window (valid) ✓
- Orange: Pre-2020 adopters specification

### File Formats
- All plots: PNG format, 300 DPI (publication quality)
- Sizes optimized for:
  - Presentations: 12" × 8" (comparison plots)
  - Papers: 10" × 6" (individual specs)
  - Supplements: 11" × 6" (cohort analysis)

## Common Questions

**Q: Why do some plots show ±24 months and others ±12?**
A: Different specifications test different event windows. All comparison plots standardize to ±12 months (the common window) for fair comparison.

**Q: Why are there gaps/missing points in some plots?**
A: Variables dropped due to collinearity (typically the last post-treatment period). This is normal and doesn't affect pre-trends tests.

**Q: Which specification should I use?**
A: **Only the narrow window (±12 months)**. It's the only one that passes pre-trends test (p=0.063).

**Q: Can I estimate long-run effects?**
A: No. Only the narrow window is valid, which limits analysis to ±12 months around treatment. Long-run estimates would be biased.

**Q: Why do early adopters have such extreme F-statistics?**
A: They have fundamentally different pre-treatment characteristics. This suggests selection into treatment based on pre-existing trends.
