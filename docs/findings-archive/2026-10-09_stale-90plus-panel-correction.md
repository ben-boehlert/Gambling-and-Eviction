# 2026-10-09 — Stale 90+ mortgage panel: today's first-pass 90+ pre-trend numbers were wrong

**What happened.** `data/processed/mortgage_treatment_panel.csv` (the 90+ day state-month panel) was built on 2026-01-23, before the 2026-08-10 treatment-schedule repair that added online dates for DE, NC, KY, ME, FL, VT and MO. Its `treatment_date` column therefore had 24 treated states instead of 31, and only 4 of the 10 June-2022 switchers (KS MD OH MA). The 30–89 panel (`mortgage3089_treatment_panel.csv`, built 2026-09-29) was on the current schedule.

**Who was affected.** Only scripts that read the panel's own `treatment_date`: the 2026-10-09 pre-trends diagnostics (`pretrends_mortgage_twfe_controls.R` 90+ comparator, `mortgage3089_pretrend_clean_controls.R` 90+ run, `mortgage_pretrend_salvage_checks.R` 90+ rows), plus the older `mortgage_delinquency_pretrends_simple.R` and `diagnostic_mortgage_data.R`. The blinded power framework (`R/power/load_blinded_panel.R`) and `analysis/main/lpdid_statepanel.R` take treatment from the schedule file and were never affected; the 2026-09-29 90+ gates, MDEs and SE probe stand.

**What was wrong, in chat and in the ledger for a few hours.** The first pass reported that the 90+ rate showed a *steeper* pre-launch decline than 30–89 on the June-2022 window (SA 0.085 ± 0.023, slope −1.4%/mo) and concluded that swapping outcomes would not salvage the mortgage design. With 4 switchers that estimate was MA/MD/OH/KS only.

**Corrected result.** Panel rebuilt with `data_prep/build_mortgage3089_panel.R` (new `MORT_FILE`/`OUT_FILE` knobs), rates bit-identical, schedule current. 90+ with the locked covariates and never-treated controls, June-2022: SA −0.019 ± 0.057, pre-only TWFE 0.036 ± 0.065, Holm 1.0 on both; spliced and pre-COVID windows likewise flat. The 2022–24 cohort's pre-launch convergence is a 30–89-only phenomenon. Ledger rows `pretrends_mortgage3089_twfe_controls` and `mortgage_pretrend_salvage_checks` and FINDINGS §2 were corrected in place the same session; the stale file is at `data/processed/deprecated/mortgage_treatment_panel_pre-schedule-repair_2026-01-23.csv`.

**Lesson.** A derived panel's treatment column is a snapshot of the schedule at build time. Diagnostics should take treatment from `utils/eviction_treatment_schedule.R` (as the framework does) or assert the panel's switcher count against the schedule before use.
