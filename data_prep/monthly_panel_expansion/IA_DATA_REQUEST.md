# Iowa SCA Data Request — Draft Email

**To:** sca@iowacourts.gov
**CC:** (your own address)
**Subject:** Data request — monthly county-level FED filings, 2016–2025

---

Dear State Court Administration,

I am a researcher at Princeton University working on a study of housing
outcomes across U.S. states. I am writing to request aggregate statistics
from Iowa Courts Online that I believe are routinely compiled by your
office.

**Specifically, I request:**

A CSV or Excel file containing monthly counts of Forcible Entry and
Detainer (FED) case filings in Iowa District Court, broken out by:

- County of filing (all 99 counties)
- Month of filing (January 2016 through the most recent complete month)
- Case type if FED is subdivided (e.g., small claims FED vs. regular FED)

I am happy to accept aggregated counts — I do not need case-level records
or any personally identifying information. If this extract is already
produced as part of SCA's internal reporting, I would appreciate the most
recent version.

**Purpose:** Academic research on the relationship between state policy
changes and housing outcomes. Results will be aggregated to the
state-month level and no individual identifying information will be used.

If there is a fee associated with producing this extract, please let me
know the estimated cost before proceeding. If a public-records request
form is required, please point me to it.

Thank you for your time. I am happy to answer any questions.

Best,
Ben Boehlert
Princeton University
bboehlert@princeton.edu
