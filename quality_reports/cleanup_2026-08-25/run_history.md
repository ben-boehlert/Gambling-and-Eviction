# Run History — Gambling and Eviction

**Summary (5 lines).** This project ran three major waves of results — a state-panel calibration wave (spring 2026, superseded by county-level work), a border/county-level diagnostic wave (2026-05-21, produced the still-live +9.1% did2s headline), and a blinded power-simulation wave for the pre-analysis plan (2026-07-23 onward, the bulk of this table). The power-sim wave was repeatedly invalidated and re-run by four corrections: the 2026-08-10 treatment-schedule repair (ME/VT/KY/FL moved from never-treated to treated), the 2026-08-20 state-panel double-count rebuild, the 2026-08-21 Nevada-exclusion audit, and the 2026-08-24 LP-DiD reweighting fix (the old `cellsize` weights were neither of the paper's two named estimands). Nearly every state-level headline number produced before 2026-08-24 is dead; the current live state spec is **shift12 + LP-DiD(ewatt or vwatt) + wild bootstrap, spliced window, NV-excluded — MDE 11.5% (ewatt) or 8.75% (vwatt)**. County-level results (did2s +9.1% headline, county CS "no finite MDE" verdict) are largely immune to all four corrections because they never touched the state panel or the NV-contaminated control pool. The pretrends headline is separately dead: a `did::att_gt` integer-`g` bug silently dropped never-treated states and inflated the ATT from the true **+0.020** to a reported **+0.080**; even the +0.020 baseline needs one more re-run to be fully current (see Milestone Timeline).

Source material: `SESSION_REPORT.md` (root, all ~1,563 lines read), `quality_reports/research_journal.md` (full), `CLAUDE.md` (root, full), `git log` on `power-cs-arm-and-shift-mode` back through 2026-06-03, and `quality_reports/plans/2026-08-25_repo-cleanup-run-ledger.md` (this task's parent plan).

1. **2026-05-21 — County-level did2s headline established.** Border/county diagnostic finds did2s is calibrated at county level (state-level CS/SunAb are not); pre-COVID drop-NH ATT = +9.1%, CI [−0.10%, +17.5%] — still the project's live eviction-filings headline.
2. **2026-07-23 — NH and Maine data-collapse artifacts repaired from court sources**, confirming the −57% NH did2s ATT and the ME zero-fill were coverage artifacts, not real effects.
3. **2026-08-03 — CS `att_gt()` found to segfault on the state panel**, blocking all state-level CS power runs for two weeks until the `cs_safe_patches`/`faster_mode` fix (2026-08-17).
4. **2026-08-10 (commit `2be0bd0`) — Treatment-schedule repair**: ME, VT, KY, FL added as treated (online dates were missing/frozen since ~early 2023); this single fix flipped state-level design feasibility mid-day and caused a "reproducibility anomaly" that took a full evening to diagnose.
5. **2026-08-17 — COVID splice identified as the actual driver of did2s/CS miscalibration**, not cohort singleton-ness or cluster count (later generalized to CS on 2026-08-19 via the time-aggregation test).
6. **2026-08-19 — County CS verdict finalized**: no defensible finite county CS MDE exists on the pre-registered grid (analytic fails size 61.2%; bootstrap arms are size-valid but flat-power). CS eliminated on power at county level; did2s remains the workhorse.
7. **2026-08-20 (01:00) — State-panel double-count discovered and rebuilt**: 12 states had ETS state-series filings summed on top of already-included county totals; invalidates essentially every pre-08-20 state-level result.
8. **2026-08-21 — Nevada-exclusion audit**: NV (always-treated since 1949, absent from the schedule CSV as a "never-legalizer") was contaminating the never-treated control pool in state-level power cells, the 2x2 pseudo-power design, era-variance, and selection diagnostics. Also this day: the pretrends `+0.080` headline traced to an integer-`g` bug in `did::att_gt` that silently dropped never-treated states (true baseline: +0.020); and `ramp75` (the handle-based treatment lag) found to be a data-vintage artifact, replaced by `shift12` (uniform launch+12mo, no outcome data).
